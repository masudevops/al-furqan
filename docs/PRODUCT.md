# Product

Al-Furqan is a tranquil, source-transparent Islamic companion centered on Quran, Tafsir, Hadith, Salah, and daily worship. It is permanently free, ad-free, and usable without registration for public religious content. The first milestone intentionally delivers Home → Quran library → authoritative Surah reader → last-read resume.

Success means a person can comfortably read the Quran on mobile or desktop with clear provenance, accessible controls, resilient error states, and no generated religious substitute. Secondary modules stay out of production navigation until their sources and functionality are complete.
